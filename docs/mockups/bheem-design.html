What changes, in one line each

  1. The thread is the whole screen: "Needs you", "Upcoming" and "Recent activity" go. A pinned strip shows the next reminder and a chip says "1 waiting" if a card is off screen.
  2. Every card sits under the answer that made it and folds into one line when done, with Undo.
  3. Buttons are verbs ("Set reminder", "Open dialer"), with a line saying what will happen.
  4. Your own records are solid bubbles; the model's words are outlined and labelled "may be wrong".
  5. Waiting is shown where the answer will land, with an honest time and Stop.
  6. A fixed Bheem look (teal and turmeric on warm paper, light and dark) instead of the wallpaper colours.

Mockup only: the real app will use the phone's own font and Material components. Small differences in spacing are expected.
